## SCREEN 8 — UNDERSTANDING INVESTMENT RISK

PURPOSE: Teach Aarav the basics before asking him to choose an investment.

Headline:
"Let's make the confusing stuff less confusing."

Create an engaging educational scene explaining:

### A. Risk and return

Higher potential returns generally involve accepting more risk. Higher returns are not guaranteed.

### B. Volatility

Investments can rise and fall. The value may be lower when the user needs to withdraw money.

### C. Diversification

Explain how spreading investments across assets or companies can reduce concentration risk, but cannot eliminate market risk.

### D. Investment horizon

Money needed soon should not automatically be placed in volatile investments.

### E. Liquidity

Explain how easily an investment can be converted to cash, including any relevant restrictions or costs.

### F. SIPs

Explain that a Systematic Investment Plan is a method of investing regularly, often monthly. A SIP is not a separate investment product and does not guarantee profit.

Keep each explanation short, visual, and interactive.

Use illustrations, simple examples, animated comparisons, and optional "learn more" controls.

Avoid long paragraphs and complicated financial jargon.

CTA:
"HELP ME FIND MY FIT ↗️"

## SCREEN 10 — DISCOVER SIP OPTIONS

PURPOSE: Help Aarav explore investment categories directly instead of leaving him with vague advice.

Headline:
"Meet your possible first investments."

Supporting copy:
"A SIP lets you invest regularly. The important part is choosing an investment that fits your goal."

Present three main educational categories.

### OPTION 1 — BROAD-MARKET INDEX FUNDS

Explain:

* They aim to track a particular market index.
* They can provide exposure to many securities through one fund.
* They still carry market risk.
* Their value can decline.

### OPTION 2 — FLEXI-CAP MUTUAL FUNDS

Explain:

* They can invest across large-, mid-, and small-cap companies.
* Their performance depends on portfolio choices and market conditions.
* They carry market risk and may underperform their benchmark.

### OPTION 3 — SMALL-CAP MUTUAL FUNDS

Explain:

* They invest primarily in smaller companies.
* They can experience substantial volatility.
* They may be unsuitable for short-term goals or users unable to tolerate significant losses.

Use Aarav's answers to decide which categories to explain in greater detail.

Do not automatically recommend small-cap funds just because the user selected a high-risk option.

For goals that require capital stability or short-term access to money, explain appropriate alternatives such as savings accounts, deposits, or other relevant lower-volatility options, including their own terms and risks.

### If actual mutual fund schemes are shown

Provide a small, curated shortlist rather than an overwhelming catalogue.

Only display real scheme names and current factual details when verified information is available.

Where available, show:

* Scheme name.
* Investment objective.
* Risk information.
* Expense ratio.
* Benchmark.
* Relevant performance data with its date.
* Why the scheme may be worth researching.
* Key limitations and risks.

Never fabricate scheme data, returns, ratings, or rankings.

Do not recommend a fund simply because it is popular or has recently performed well.

Make it possible to compare options and read more before proceeding.

"EXPLORE THIS OPTION"

## Animation language

Use the existing animation library and implementation.

Create:

* Smooth left-to-right scene transitions.
* Staggered text reveals.
* Subtle character reactions.
* Spring-like button interactions.
* Animated currency values.
* Interactive budget blocks.
* Animated coin and savings visuals.
* Interactive chart transitions.
* A tasteful celebration.

Avoid excessive motion, distracting animations, and transitions that prevent users from interacting.

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Responsive design

Desktop:

* Large illustrations and expressive layouts.
* Strong composition and generous negative space.

Mobile:

* Recompose scenes vertically.
* Keep Aarav recognisable.
* Use large touch targets.
* Keep currency inputs and financial information readable.
* Prevent horizontal overflow.

## Accessibility

Implement:

* Semantic HTML.
* Keyboard navigation.
* Visible focus states.
* Accessible form labels.
* Appropriate colour contrast.
* Clear validation and error states.
* Reduced-motion support.
